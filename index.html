🇬🇧 English — Start here

The goal is not to fill the app with data. Use it to turn study into measurable results.

1
Set up your examOpen Settings → choose A/L or O/L → add your exam date → add subjects → set the past-paper year range.
2
Build your Study PlanAdd each lesson and break it into small sub-topics. Tick a sub-topic only when you actually understand or complete it.
3
Study and produce an outputDo questions, finish a sub-topic, complete a section, or solve a paper. The important part is what you produced—not just time spent.
4
Log the sessionRecord the subject, hours, score/result and what went wrong. This makes your dashboard reflect real work.
5
Use Past Papers as evidenceEnter MCQ, SEQ and EQ marks. Watch the full-paper trend and component chart to see whether your performance is improving.
6
Fix weaknessesPut repeated mistakes into Weakness Vault. Give each weakness a next revision action, then mark it resolved only after you can handle it.
7
Check the Dashboard dailyUse the countdown, progress, study-time chart, score trends and top weaknesses to decide what to do next.

🔁 The StudyFlow loop / StudyFlow වැඩ කරන cycle එක

හැම study cycle එකකම මේ flow එක repeat කරන්න. Plan → Do → Measure → Fix → Repeat.

1. PLAN
මොනවද කරන්නෙ?
2. DO
ඇත්තටම කරන්න
3. MEASURE
Score / result
4. FIX
Weakness fix
5. REPEAT
ආයෙ check කරන්න

✅ How to improve productivity / Productivity වැඩි කරගන්න

  • Start with one clear task instead of “study everything”.
  • End each session with an output: questions solved, a completed sub-topic, a paper section, or a better score.
  • Use the Weakness Vault to decide what deserves revision next.
  • Use study hours to measure consistency, but use scores and completed work to measure effectiveness.
  • Before finishing the day, decide the next small action so you can start faster tomorrow.
